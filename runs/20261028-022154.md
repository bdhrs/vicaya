Cause: measuring the collection as data before searching for opinions. Fix: preserve. Scope: global.
- [POSITIVE] Evidence: the second, source-armed reviewer caught two argument-breaking errors (the Ud 6.4 verse does exist in the Sanskrit; the Sanskrit versified the prose udānas rather than dropping them) that the external cross-check had also half-missed. Cause: giving the reviewer the helpers and asking it to resolve every doubt. Fix: preserve. Scope: global.

Cause: assuming `paranum` was populated on every row. - Suggest: extend `check-quotes` to pair the template's `- Pāḷi: "…"` bullet form (it currently sees only blockquotes), or have `validate_note.py` warn when the count of Pāḷi bullet entries exceeds the number of quotes `check-quotes` tested.
- Suggest: move `scratch-check-coverage --note <draft>` into the Phase 5 checklist as a required step immediately after writing the draft body, before the Devil's Advocate answers — a library source found there changed an argument this run.
- Suggest: add to the Phase 5 Devil's Advocate checklist a question requiring the thesis to be restated in the collection's own unit terms (here: utterance vs frame, not verse vs prose), since the user's phrasing can import a false dichotomy.
- Suggest: document that `paranum` is empty on `gatha` rows in the Udāna table (as for continuation rows elsewhere), and that a lexicographic `MAX()` forward-fill fails on multi-digit numbers — use a correlated subquery.
