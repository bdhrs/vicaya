## Summary

This run produced a full comparative study of modern empirical consciousness research (reincarnation case studies, NDE research, regression hypnosis) against the EBT account of *punabbhava* and the 31-plane cosmology. The central finding is that the two sides share a claim — "rebirth occurs" — but are structurally divergent on every methodological and philosophical dimension: personal identity vs. *anattā*, 31-realm cosmology vs. human-to-human sampling, meditative *cutūpapāta-ñāṇa* vs. third-person empirical method, liberation from the cycle vs. intellectual understanding.

The note identifies DN1 para 31-33 as a key but often-overlooked EBT passage: the Buddha explicitly warns that past-life recollection leads practitioners to infer eternal selfhood — the same inference modern reincarnation research invites, and the EBT's own internal critique of it.

## Key findings

1. **Convergence on the fact of rebirth**: Both the EBT and the case research affirm that something meaningful is transmitted across lives; they differ entirely on what that "something" is.

2. **Anattā as the central divergence**: MN38 Sāti episode makes the EBT's position unambiguous — no self-identical consciousness crosses lives. Stevenson's personal-survival framework, Weiss's soul-plans, and Cannon's between-lives realm all presuppose exactly what MN38 rejects.

3. **DN1 eternalism warning**: The *Brahmajālasutta* paras 31-33 anticipate the Stevenson-type move: past-life recollection → inference of permanent self = the first form of *sassatavāda*. This is a critical EBT self-check on the research paradigm.

4. **Cosmological scope mismatch**: Modern research samples human-to-human (and occasionally animal-to-human). The EBT's 31-realm system has vast territories — niraya, peta, deva, brahma realms — that no modern research programme has accessed or addressed.

5. **Epistemological incommensurability**: The EBT authorizes only deep-*samādhi* meditative knowledge (*cutūpapāta-ñāṇa*) as reliable access to rebirth facts. Neither case research nor NDE phenomenology nor regression induction meets this standard. MN60's *apaṇṇaka* argument is the EBT's alternative for non-meditators: rational inference under uncertainty, not empirical proof.

6. **Regression hypnosis is the weakest programme**: Epistemological (no samādhi basis), methodological (suggestibility, confabulation), and cosmological (intermediate realm contradicts Theravāda) problems — all three.
