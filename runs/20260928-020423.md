- [WORKFLOW] Evidence: three separate CST paragraph-number misattributions were caught only by fetching the owning paranum: DN33 §327 is "six unsurpassed things" not the six recollections (which is DN34 §356); DN33 §331's first row is the seven grounds for blamelessness and the seven saññā is a continuation row; Vibhaṅga §300 was the kasiṇa formula, not the objectless one (§205). Cause: the helper's `id` and `paranum` fields both appear in dumped rows and are both small integers. Fix: when a citation needs a section number, call resolve-citation with the para value taken from the search output, never from a row id. Scope: local (run note) / global (worth a line in the scratchpad section).
