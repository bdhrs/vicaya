## Retrospective
- [POSITIVE] Evidence: resolving `anudayā` to `anuddayā` in DPD before Phase 0 turned a suspected transcription error into a real finding — 12 T1a rows, and the everyday-sympathy half of the question survived. Cause: checked the dictionary before declaring ambiguity. Fix: keep doing the DPD check on any doubtful term rather than asking the user. Scope: global.
- [POSITIVE] Evidence: the source-armed reviewer caught three substantive errors the external cross-check missed (AN4.125's Ābhassara mapping asserted against the draft's own rule; the Visuddhimagga's *attani* position reversed; Puggalapaññatti §35 vs §121). Cause: it could run `get-paragraph`. Fix: none. Scope: global.
- [WORKFLOW] Evidence: the frequency sentence went out with "four Nikāyas" attached to a whole-stratum count, and the reviewer's own corrected figures were themselves wrong (citation forms, not stems). Cause: two different counting bases in one sentence, neither labelled. Fix: name the basis (stem vs citation form) and the scope (four Nikāyas vs stratum) in the same sentence. Scope: global.
- [CONFUSION] Evidence: spent several turns on elided brahmavihāra formulas — most Nikāya occurrences read `karuṇāsahagatena cetasā…pe…`, so a "complete" quote for compassion is scarce and the risk of attributing an upekkhā sentence to compassion is high. Cause: the CST text preserves the abbreviation. Fix: treat `…pe…` attribution as an inference to be declared, never as a quote. Scope: global.
- [BEHAVIOR] Evidence: the temp dir was created and used correctly and no scratch hand-edits raced helper calls. Nothing to report. Cause: followed the lock rule. Fix: none. Scope: local.

## Improvement suggestions
- Suggest: add a note to the template that a "What is not in the suttas" section is worth including when the question is a survey, since absence claims are the highest-risk claims and a dedicated section forces them out of the prose.
- Suggest: in the "Quote fully, not representatively" paragraph, add that CST preserves the brahmavihāra formula's `…pe…` abbreviation, so an "unabbreviated" quote often exists in only two or three tables — and that attributing an abbreviated clause to one member of a set is an inference that must be declared.
- Suggest: when a reviewer supplies corrected frequency figures, re-derive them from the same sweep the note used (stem vs citation form differ by an order of magnitude: `anukamp` 138 four-Nikāya rows, `anukampā` 12).
- Suggest: the self-audit question about "cross-check corrections" would be sharper if it asked specifically whether the reviewer's *own* corrections were verified, not only the draft's claims.
